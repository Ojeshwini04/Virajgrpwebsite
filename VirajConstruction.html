About Viraj Group

A single point of contact from planning to handover

Established in 2023, Viraj Group is an emerging, entrepreneur-driven company in the civil industry, committed to comprehensive end-to-end solutions with a strong focus on quality, efficiency and client satisfaction.

Our expertise spans architectural design, detailing, civil and structural work, electrical, plumbing, painting, tiling, stainless steel railings, woodwork, interiors, doors & windows, wall art and home automation.

We work with trusted, reputed brands to supply quality materials while also offering budget-friendly options.
